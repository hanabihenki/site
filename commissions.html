Commission Information

  • Payment is in USD and is available through PayPal and Ko-Fi. PayPal automatically converts other currencies to USD.
  • Payment can be done any time — upfront, after the sketch, after the un-shaded phase, or after the whole illustration is done. Time of payment is up to you! (Payment must be upfront for payment-dependent commissions, such as a "pay what you want" commission.)
  • 18+ illustrations and animations are available, but you'll have to contact me to see examples.
  • Preferred communication channels, most to least preferred: Discord, Reddit, Fur Affinity, Instagram.
