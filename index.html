Intro 𓆩✮𓆪

Hi, my name is Ray! (I also go by Hanabihenki, Azurazar, and Raydrawsx online.) I'm a Japanese-American artist with skills in illustration, 2D and 3D animation, 3D modeling, music production, and front-end programming. I have a passion for storytelling and like to tell a story with every piece of art I make.

My work online is varied in style and messaging. I like to explore satirization and commentary with my artwork. Please note that art I upload online is not a representation of my opinions, unless otherwise stated.
